----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 — Open the Shop Page
  Example URL: /bookme/my-barber-shop
----------------------------------------------------------------

  What to do:
    Open the link shared by the business.

  What you should see:
    ✔  The shop's banner photo at the top.
    ✔  Business name, address, star rating and review count.
    ✔  Icons linking to Instagram, Google Maps, Website (if set).
    ✔  A green dot badge "Seats available today" if the shop has
       open slots today.
    ✔  Three tabs at the bottom: Services | Portfolio | About.
    ✔  Services tab is open by default.

  On the Services tab:
    ✔  A toggle at the top: [ Onsite ] and [ Mobile ].
       - Onsite = services you visit the shop for.
       - Mobile = services where the barber comes to you.
    ✔  A search bar — type a service name to filter the list.
    ✔  Each service row shows the name and price.
    ✔  Tap a row to expand it and see available time slots.
    ✔  Walk-in services show staff working hours instead of slots.

  Checks to do:
    □  Page loads without a blank white screen.
    □  Tapping Portfolio shows images/videos (or "No content" message).
    □  Tapping About shows opening hours and staff names.
    □  Switching Onsite ↔ Mobile changes which services are shown.
    □  Search bar filters correctly and clearing it restores the list.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2A — Pick a Time (via "View other times" link)
  URL: /bookme/{slug}/view-times
----------------------------------------------------------------

  What to do:
    Expand a service row and tap "View other times →".

  What you should see:
    ✔  A list of staff at the top — "Anyone" is pre-selected.
    ✔  A horizontal date strip — today is highlighted.
    ✔  A grid of available time slots below.
    ✔  A "Book Appointment" button at the bottom (greyed out until
       you pick a time).

  What to try:
    □  Tap a different staff member — slots should refresh.
    □  Tap a different date — slots should refresh; previously
       picked time clears.
    □  Past dates should be greyed out / not tappable.
    □  Tap a time slot — it should highlight and the button
       should turn active (gold/coloured).
    □  If no slots exist for a date: "No availability for this
       date – try another day" message should appear.
    □  Tap "Book Appointment" — goes to Confirm Booking page.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2B — Quick Book (tap a slot directly on the shop page)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  What to do:
    Instead of "View other times", just tap a time slot pill
    directly inside the service row on the shop page.

  What should happen:
    ✔  You are taken straight to Confirm Booking (skips Step 2A).
    ✔  The slot chosen is always for today.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 3 — Confirm Booking Page
  URL: /bookme/{slug}/confirm-booking
----------------------------------------------------------------

  What to do:
    Arrive here after picking a time in Step 2A or 2B.

  STAGE 1 — Review your booking:
    ✔  A summary card shows:
       Business Name | Service | Staff | Date & Time | Duration | Total
    ✔  A cancellation policy note below the card.
    ✔  Tap "Looks good – let's lock it in" to continue.

  STAGE 2 — Fill in your details:

    ✔  Form fields appear:
       - Your Name (required)
       - "Booking for someone else?" toggle (shows guest name field)
       - Country code picker (defaults to Australia +61)
       - Phone Number (required)
       - Email (optional)
       - Drop-off Address (only shown for Mobile service type)

    ✔  If you saved details last time, they are pre-filled and a
       banner says "Using your saved info". Tap Clear to remove.

    Payment options (choose one):
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  Pay on site   — pay cash/card when you arrive           │
    │  Apple/Google Pay — pay via your phone's wallet          │
    │  Card          — enter card details now                  │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    PAY ON SITE:
    □  No extra fields appear.
    □  Button says "Confirm Booking" and becomes active when
       name and phone are filled.

    APPLE / GOOGLE PAY:
    □  Apple Pay / Google Pay button appears if your browser
       supports it.
    □  If not supported: message "not available on this device".

    CARD:
    □  A secure card input box appears (Stripe).
    □  "Name on card" field appears.
    □  Button only activates after card details are complete.

  Tapping "Confirm Booking":
    □  Button shows "Processing…" and cannot be tapped again.
    □  On success: success pop-up appears (see Step 4).
    □  On error: red error message appears below the button.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 4 — Success Pop-up (appears on Confirm Booking page)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  What you should see after a successful booking:
    ✔  A pop-up slides up over a blurred background.
    ✔  "You're all set, [Your Name]" greeting.
    ✔  Your 4-digit Confirmation PIN (show this at the shop).
    ✔  "We've sent a text with your booking link" message.

  Buttons inside the pop-up:
    ✔  "Add to Calendar" — downloads a .ics calendar file.
       □  Button shows "Downloading…" while it works.
       □  Your calendar app should open / offer to import the event.
    ✔  "Save details for faster booking?" banner:
       □  Tap Yes → details saved; banner shows "Details saved ✓".
       □  Tap X  → banner dismissed, nothing saved.
    ✔  "Back to [Shop Name]" — returns to the shop page.

  Checks:
    □  The background behind the pop-up is blurred.
    □  The pop-up itself is sharp and readable.
    □  Tapping outside the pop-up does NOT close it accidentally.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 5 — View / Cancel a Booking
  URL: /bookme/{slug}/booking/{id}  (from SMS link)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  What to do:
    Open the booking link sent via SMS after your booking.

  What you should see:
    ✔  Booking card showing:
       Service | Provider | Date | Time | Duration |
       Payment Method | Price | Verification Code | Status
    ✔  Status badge: "Upcoming" (green) or "Cancelled" (red/grey).

  To cancel:
    □  Tap the cancel option (only visible for Upcoming bookings).
    □  A confirmation step appears — tap Confirm to proceed.
    □  Status badge updates to "Cancelled" immediately.
    □  An error message appears if cancellation fails.


----------------------------------------------------------------

  □  Both pages should load without error.
  □  Back button should take you to the previous page.

  QUICK CHECKLIST — Full Booking Run-Through

  Use this for a fast end-to-end smoke test:

  □  1. Open /bookme/{slug} — shop page loads correctly.
  □  2. Switch to Mobile mode — service list updates.
  □  3. Search for a service — results filter correctly.
  □  4. Expand a service row — slots or hours appear.
  □  5. Tap "View other times" — view-times page loads.
  □  6. Change staff and date — slots refresh each time.
  □  7. Select a time slot — "Book Appointment" button activates.
  □  8. Tap "Book Appointment" — confirm page loads with summary.
  □  9. Tap "Looks good" — booking form appears.
  □ 10. Fill in name + phone — Confirm button activates.
  □ 11. Choose payment method and tap Confirm — processing state shown.
  □ 12. Success pop-up appears — PIN visible, background is blurred.
  □ 13. Tap "Add to Calendar" — file downloads.
  □ 14. Tap "Save details" → Yes — banner shows "Details saved ✓".
  □ 15. Tap "Back to [Shop]" — returns to shop page.
  □ 16. Open booking SMS link — booking details page loads.
  □ 17. Cancel the booking — status changes to Cancelled.


================================================================
  THINGS THAT SHOULD NEVER HAPPEN
================================================================

  ✗  White blank screen on any page.
  ✗  Page redirecting you away unexpectedly (e.g. back to home).
  ✗  Confirm button working twice (double booking).
  ✗  Booking created without showing a PIN.
  ✗  Card details visible in browser's network requests.
  ✗  Pop-up itself appearing blurred.
  ✗  Past dates being selectable in the calendar.
  ✗  Walk-in service showing bookable time slots.
  ✗  Price showing $0 for a paid service.
